Definitions:

- **FM-01** Compass drafts a hardship offer before the borrower has confirmed current income.
- **FM-02** The borrower asked for a person and no loan officer took over.
- **FM-03** A hardship offer reaches the borrower without the sentence that a loan officer must approve it.
- **FM-04** Compass offers a deferral to a borrower who had one in the last twelve months.
- **FM-05** Compass quotes the retired ten-day grace period; policy is fifteen days.
- **FM-06** Compass explains a late fee that policy says should be waived.
- **FM-07** The balance Compass quotes is dated wrongly.
- **FM-08** Compass answers about one loan when the borrower has two.
- **FM-09** The payoff quote leaves out daily interest.
- **FM-10** The payoff quote's good-through date is wrong.
- **FM-11** Compass promises the lien release sooner than the title office can record it.
- **FM-12** The release goes to, or names, the wrong state's title office.
- **FM-13** The statement sent covers a different period from the one asked for.
